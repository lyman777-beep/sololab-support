Privacy Policy

FeeCapture — Effective Date: September 4, 2026

Summary: FeeCapture stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Your data never leaves your device.

1. Information We Collect

FeeCapture does not collect any personal information. The app does not require account creation or login, and we do not operate servers.

The app processes the following data entirely on your device:

  • Client information you enter (name, phone number, email, company, notes)
  • Call details and billable time you record
  • Billing records and amounts
  • Settings, fee types, and preferences

You may optionally import a contact from your address book to create a client; the imported name and phone number are copied to your device only.

2. How Your Data Is Used

All data is processed locally on your device to:

  • Calculate billable amounts from the time and rate you enter
  • Show captured revenue on your Dashboard
  • Generate CSV and PDF exports that you initiate
  • Save your clients, records, and preferences

No data is transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). Data remains on your device until you delete the app or reset data from within Settings. Uninstalling the app permanently removes locally stored data.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled and processed on your device by iOS. They are not sent to us or to any server.

5. Subscriptions and Purchases

Optional subscription plans are processed through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment processing and subscription management. We do not have access to your payment information. FeeCapture only receives a basic entitlement status.

6. Third-Party Services

FeeCapture does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

7. Children's Privacy

FeeCapture does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for adult professionals.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
