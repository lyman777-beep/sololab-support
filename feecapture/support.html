Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log a billable call?

Open the Quick Record tab, pick a client, set the duration and rate, and make sure "Billable" is on. The amount is calculated automatically. Tap Save when you're done.

How is the amount calculated?

The app multiplies the billable time by the rate, using the billing unit (hourly or per-minute) and rounding rule (exact, up to 6 minutes, or up to 15 minutes) for the client or your default settings.

What is Pro?

Pro adds up to 200 records per month and 50 clients, CSV/PDF export, and more. Premium offers unlimited records and clients. All paid plans are billed through the App Store.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → FeeCapture → Cancel. You can also manage it at apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Does the app upload my data?

No. All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Does it work on Android?

FeeCapture is currently available on iOS. Android support is planned for a future release.
