Open the Quick Record tab, pick a client, set the duration and rate, and make sure "Billable" is on. The amount is calculated automatically — just add a fee type or note and tap Save.

The app multiplies the billable time by the rate, applying the billing unit (hourly or per-minute) and rounding rule (exact, up to 6 minutes, or up to 15 minutes) configured for the client or as your default.

The Dashboard shows this month's captured revenue, today's and this week's totals (grouped per currency), your active clients, and your five most recent records.
