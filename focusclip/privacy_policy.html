Privacy Policy

FocusClip — Effective Date: September 10, 2026

In short: All FocusClip data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no analytics, no ads, and no tracking — and we never sell data. Payments are handled by Apple; we receive only your subscription validity status.

1. Information We Collect

FocusClip collects no personal information. The app requires no account, registration is not required, and we do not operate any servers.

The app processes the following data locally on your device only:

  • Bedtime schedule, quiet mode, and reminder settings
  • Nightly sessions and settlement records (outcome, violation counts, mode used)
  • Streaks, points, stars, constellations, cloud cards, achievements, and collection (local)
  • Shielding rules: category templates, handpicked apps, and (in Strict mode and for subscribers) whole categories, web domains, and allowances
  • Exported files (generated locally, only when you initiate them)

FamilyControls / Screen Time capabilities are used solely to limit high-disturbance content on your own device. We do not read messages, and we do not perform lock-screen detection, screen-unlock statistics, or unlock counting.

2. How Data Is Used

All data is processed locally on your device and is used to:

  • Switch into quiet mode and limit high-disturbance content during the bedtime hours you set
  • Settle automatically the next morning and generate your nightly summary, receipt, and violation details
  • Track stars, constellations, cloud cards, achievements, and streaks, and show your collection wall and year-in-nights
  • Generate export files you initiate (monthly keepsake / full history export)
  • Grant local perseverance rewards such as the gifted month

Data is never transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's local storage (SwiftData / App Group: group.com.focusclip.app). Deleting the app deletes its local data. You can also erase all local data in the app's Settings. After uninstalling or erasing your device, we have no way to restore your data — please keep your own backup of anything important.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled and delivered locally by iOS. They are never sent to us or to any server.

5. Subscriptions & Purchases

Optional subscriptions are processed and billed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment processing and subscription management; we receive only a basic entitlement status confirming whether your subscription is active. We have no access to your payment information and never collect card or other payment data.

6. Third-Party Services

FocusClip uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

7. Children's Privacy

FocusClip is designed for personal self-management by the device owner and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is not a parental-control or enterprise-supervision tool.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
